# M7 — Add-ons ACM, Argo CD and GitLab

Install Red Hat Advanced Cluster Management, Argo CD and GitLab through
add-ons. The [product scope](../project.md#product-scope) names the three
packages among the cluster-bound integrations. Custom-catalog acquisition and
the custom package lifecycle belong here only if one of them ships as a custom
package.

**Requires:** M5, for the package lifecycle of [B83](m5.md#b83). **Exit tier:**
an emulated rehearsal until B91's definition fixes it. **Open decisions:**
built-in or custom for each package, which decides whether B89 and B90 are
needed.

## Item detail

### B89

Custom-catalog acquisition, immutable publication, selection and removal,
including the storage location and record format of `add-ons add`
registrations and the meaning of the
[`add-ons/_store` selection exception](../api/environment.md#resource-and-cluster-selection).
No source, trust, storage or selection contract exists yet, and the three
`add-ons` commands stay unavailable until this item is promoted. **Exit
evidence:** closed schemas and formats, fixed bounds, authenticity, atomic and
crash-safe storage, deterministic selection, retention through destroy and
security and acceptance tests.

### B90

**Exit evidence:** exact identities, a qualified driver, the host-contract
suite, code-content refusal and apply, readiness, replay and destroy
acceptance.
