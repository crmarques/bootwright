# M6 — OpenShift clusters over OpenShift Virtualization

Provision and destroy OpenShift clusters whose nodes are KubeVirt virtual
machines under OpenShift Virtualization. The
[product scope](../project.md#product-scope) names KubeVirt, including
OpenShift Virtualization, and OpenShift container clusters. The KubeVirt
provider refuses before registration today
([refused machine arms](../deferred/machine-arms.md)); lifting that refusal
needs its own slice.

**Requires:** M3. **Exit tier:** an emulated rehearsal until B87's definition
fixes it. **Open decisions:** none numbered.

### B87

arm is a capability package plus one case of the
[target derivation](../substrates.md#selection-and-refusal), and no consumer of
a realized Machine changes. Its vSphere counterpart is parked as
[B99](backlog.md#b99). **Exit evidence:** the exact release, adapter contract,
its two fixed identity and pre-boot task files, failure and replay tests and
real-system qualification; [refused machine arms](../deferred/machine-arms.md)
